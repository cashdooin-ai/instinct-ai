Privacy policy

Draft for the pilot. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before public launch. Last updated: October 2026.

What Zyyko does

Zyyko helps people in Chandigarh Tricity get offers from nearby shops and service providers through WhatsApp and zyyko.com.

What we collect

  • Buyers: your WhatsApp number and profile name, the messages and voice notes you send us, the area or location you share, and the offers you choose.
  • Shops: shop name, owner name, phone number, location, categories, the offers you send, and ratings from buyers.
  • Website: details you enter in our sign-up form.

How we use it

  • To send your request to suitable nearby shops and send their offers back to you.
  • When you choose an offer, we share your name and phone number with that shop only so they can contact you, and share the shop's contact with you.
  • To improve matching (for example, shop response speed and ratings) and to help when something goes wrong.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use WhatsApp (Meta) to send and receive messages, cloud hosting and database providers to run the service, an AI provider to understand messages, and a speech-to-text provider for voice notes. They process data only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep request and offer records for up to 24 months to resolve disputes and improve the service, then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

Your choices

Send STOP to cancel a request at any time. To access, correct or delete your data, message us on WhatsApp or email privacy@zyyko.com (set up this mailbox before launch).
