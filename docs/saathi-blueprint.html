Instinct for Bharat: call it, text it, it gets done

Instinct proved that people will hand errands to an AI agent that texts like a friend. It is US-only, invite-only, burns compute running a cloud computer per user, and has privacy problems. This blueprint designs an Indian version, working name Saathi. It is built on India's own rails (UPI, ONDC, BBPS, DigiLocker, merchant MCPs) and on voice calls in Indian languages. Human runners cover the jobs India needs most and Instinct never planned for: sarkari paperwork, refund chasing, and looking after parents from afar.

Working name only. Figures marked "reported" come from press coverage. Figures marked "assumption" are mine; change them in the calculator.

How Instinct works

Instinct is built by Spear Street Technology in San Francisco, founded in 2025 by Noah Shinn (23, ex-Sierra). You text it on iMessage or WhatsApp, or call it. It works through your connected email, calendar, messages, screen, location, stored credentials and payment details. It does the work on its own cloud phone and computer, using apps and websites the way a person would. It books travel, orders groceries, checks you into flights, makes reservations and follows up on threads you dropped.

How it plans to make money

  • Free to users. Shinn has said he wants to deliver it "for free to everyone for a lifetime." He has explicitly rejected ads.
  • Merchant commission, like Apple Pay. Merchants pay for delivered customers. Shinn cites boutique hotels paying up to 30% per booking. Travel is half of volume, so this is the core engine.
  • Instinct Concierge (17 Sep 2026): a "white-glove" layer that places phone calls, for example booking restaurants without online reservations, joining a dentist's cancellation list, or fixing a cable bill. It is in early access. Meta Muse shipped calling the same day.
  • Instinct Selections (late Sep 2026): human-curated lists for dining, travel and shopping from "local chefs, designers, travel guides." Users pushed back on unsolicited suggestions for luggage, sunglasses and hats. Whether these are monetised has not been disclosed.
  • Possible paid tiers. Third-party reports estimate $200–500 a month tied to cloud-desktop compute. Its terms reserve the right to charge.

Most of the new money goes to compute. Shinn reportedly spends about 40% of his time securing it. That is the price of the cloud computer per user design.

Critical constraint: WhatsApp, cross-checked

The rule. Since 15 Jan 2026 (15 Oct 2025 for new accounts), the WhatsApp Business Solution terms bar "AI Providers" when a general-purpose assistant is the "primary (rather than incidental or ancillary) functionality." ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, Luzia and Poke were removed. Bots for bookings, orders, support and notifications are allowed. Regulators forced exceptions only in Italy, Brazil and the EU: the EU ordered free access restored on 9 Jun 2026 and ChatGPT returned in the EEA on 13 Jul 2026. No regulator has acted in India, so the ban applies here.

What Instinct actually does. A real chat with Instinct's number shows "Business Account" and "This business is now using a secure service from Meta to manage this chat." That is Meta's official Cloud API. An unlinked number gets a link to app.instinct.com/whatsapp-onboarding to sign up. So Instinct uses WhatsApp as its front door, with the account, connections and payments on its own web app. It has not been blocked as of Sept 2026. Our reading, which is unconfirmed: Instinct either presents itself as an errand and booking service where AI is how it delivers (the "ancillary" side of the rule), or Meta hasn't enforced against it yet. Poke, a very similar agent, was cut off, and Instinct competes head-on with Meta's own Muse, so this is a live platform risk for Instinct too.

What Saathi should do. Use the WhatsApp Cloud API the same way: an errand service with structured flows (task confirmation, quotes, UPI payment links, status updates, receipts) and the account on our app. Don't market it as "ChatGPT on WhatsApp." User-started service chats are free, so WhatsApp is the cheapest channel in India. But never depend on it alone: run the phone number, Android app and RCS in parallel from day one so a WhatsApp cut-off is an inconvenience, not the end of the company. Get a written policy opinion from a Meta Business Solution Provider before launch.

The India play: seven ways to beat them

Positioning: "Saathi gets it done, even when it takes ten phone calls and two weeks." Instinct is a concierge for affluent Americans. Saathi is a persistent fixer for Indian families.

1. WhatsApp plus voice, with no single point of failure

WhatsApp is the front door, as it is for Instinct: voice notes in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali and more, with structured confirmations and UPI links. The same phone number also takes calls, so parents can use it without an app. The Android app and RCS mirror everything in case Meta enforces its AI-provider rule.

vs Instinct: English text, one platform risk

2. API-first task router

Route each task to the cheapest path that works: merchant MCP/API, then ONDC/BBPS/UPI, then browser automation, then an outbound call, then a human runner. Most Indian tasks never need a cloud computer.

vs Instinct: a cloud computer for everything, compute-capped

3. The "sarkari and refunds" desk

Chase airline and e-commerce refunds, insurance claims, electricity and telecom complaints, passport, RTO and Aadhaar appointments. Charge a success fee: pay only when the money or slot arrives.

vs Instinct: travel and restaurants

4. Abroad plan for NRIs looking after parents

About 18M Indians abroad worry about parents at home. "Book Papa's blood test, pay the society bill, send a plumber." Priced in USD, with human runners for physical errands.

The highest-ARPU segment and nobody owns it

5. UPI Reserve Pay, not stored cards

Use NPCI's agentic UPI (Reserve Pay mandates with per-merchant caps that can be revoked instantly). Saathi never holds card numbers or passwords.

vs Instinct: stored credentials and payments

6. Privacy you can see

Ask permission per task, show a readable action log, and when you disconnect, delete everything (with proof). Be DPDP-ready before the May 2027 deadline and integrate a registered consent manager.

vs Instinct: kept data after disconnect, perpetual-licence terms

7. Honest commissions

Every suggestion shows "Saathi earns ₹X from this" and a cheaper option if one exists. Recommendations only when asked. Indian users are sceptical of deals, and transparency builds word of mouth.

vs Instinct: the Selections backlash

Roadmap

  1. Days 0–90 (MVP, one city, Hindi + English): WhatsApp Cloud API front door (structured flows) plus a phone number and Android app, voice notes in and calls out, the four MVP jobs above, a human ops desk of 5–8 people, UPI collect/intent payments. Closed beta of about 2,000 users through referrals. Target 70% of tasks fully automated and a measured cost per task.
  2. Months 4–9 (Phase 2): 6 more languages, travel and the sarkari desk, UPI Reserve Pay mandates once NPCI's Unified Agent Protocol opens, Family and Abroad plans, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Pune.
  3. Months 10–18 (Phase 3): SMB "agent-ready" network, insurance and finance partners, B2B API so banks and telcos can white-label Saathi, Tier-2 cities.

Architecture: the cheapest path that works

Revenue model

India will not pay $200 a month, and free AI is everywhere (ChatGPT Go, Gemini via Jio). So we charge for outcomes and persistence, which free chatbots don't deliver, and we never sell ads.

Next 30 days

  1. Talk to 50 users in three groups: NRIs with parents in India, working urban couples, and people aged 50+. Ask about the last five errands that cost them over an hour.
  2. Run a "Wizard of Oz" pilot. A phone number plus 3 people doing tasks by hand with AI help for 200 users. Log every task with time, route and cost.
  3. Prototype the router using Swiggy MCP, a BBPS partner sandbox and Indic STT/TTS, and measure real cost per task for each path.
  4. Start partner conversations: Razorpay (agentic UPI), an ONDC buyer-app network participant, a BBPS operating unit, an IRCTC-authorised agent.
  5. Legal groundwork: DLT registration for outbound calls, a DPDP privacy design review, and a written WhatsApp usage policy that keeps us inside the Business API rules.
